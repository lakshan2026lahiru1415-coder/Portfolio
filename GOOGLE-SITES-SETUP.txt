Lahiru Lakshan | ED4023 teaching portfolio

1. Extract this ZIP. Upload the extracted folder to your HTTPS static website host.
   Keep index.html at the site root with the case pages and assets folder alongside it.
2. Open the hosted homepage and check that your intended viewers can open the CV
   and project PDFs. The host must allow embedding on your Google Site.
3. In Google Sites, open Pages > Add (+) > Full page embed. Paste the hosted
   homepage URL, choose Insert, and name the page Teaching portfolio.
4. Preview on phone and desktop, then publish and check the published page
   with the same access as an assessor.

Google Sites takes the hosted page URL in the full-page embed field.
Google Sites Help: https://support.google.com/sites/answer/90569?hl=en
