Teaching approach

I want students to be able to point to the part of a model or a calculation that explains their answer. That is why I keep work in progress alongside finished examples. In the scale drawing, the first measurement tells me more about the final dimensions than the neatness of the sketch.

I selected each sample for a decision a reader can inspect. In each case I describe the mathematical check, what the sample shows, and one change I would try in a later lesson.
